# Platform rules

## r/SideProject
- **Goal:** honest feedback and bug reports from builders who will actually try it.
- **Title:** what you built + an invitation, e.g. "I built X that does Y. Roast it."
- **Body:** the problem in one line → what it does (3 bullets) → what's free → 2–3 specific questions ("did the output feel generic?", "anything broken on mobile?") → link → "I'll reply to every comment today."
- Include a screenshot or GIF. No marketing adjectives.
- Read the sidebar rules first. Reply within an hour. Don't cross-post the same text to other subreddits.

## Show HN
- **Title:** `Show HN: <Name> – <what it does in plain words>`, 80 characters max. No superlatives, no exclamation marks.
- **It must be possible to try it without signing up**, or a sample output has to be linked. Otherwise, flag it as a blocker.
- **Author's first comment** (post it right away): why you built it → how it works technically → what's free → 1–2 things you're genuinely unsure about → stack → "happy to answer anything."
- Never ask anyone for upvotes; HN penalises voting rings. Answer criticism with facts.

## Product Hunt
- **Launch time:** 12:01am PT, Tue–Thu, scheduled ahead of time.
- **Tagline:** 60 characters max. **Description:** 260 characters max.
- **Topics:** 3–4 relevant ones.
- **Gallery shot list:** 4–6 images in order. The first one shows the product's core change (before → after).
- **Maker comment:** a short hello → why it exists → an offer for PH visitors (free tier or `[discount code]`) → a question that invites replies.
- Ask your network to "check it out and leave feedback," never to "upvote." Reply to every comment all day.

## Indie Hackers
- **Goal:** a build story. The product is the context, not the headline.
- **Title:** about a lesson or a result, e.g. "I launched on X and Y this week. Here's what broke."
- **Body:** why this problem → the business model and pricing → `[real numbers]` from the launch → what you got wrong → what you're changing → a specific question for founders → link.
- Also add the product to the IH Products directory. Comment on other people's posts that week.

## Uneed
- A short directory listing: name, tagline, a 1–2 sentence description, category, pricing model.
- Pick the next free launch slot (or pay to skip the queue). On launch day, share the Uneed page.
- After launch, check that the backlink to the site is live and dofollow.
