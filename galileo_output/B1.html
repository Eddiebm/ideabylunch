Why this exists

Most coffee has forgotten where it came from.

Somewhere between the farm and your cup, coffee usually loses its story. It gets blended into anonymity, roasted in industrial volumes, and sold without a word about who grew it. The flavour suffers — and so do the people doing the hardest work.

Anonymous origins

A country name on a bag tells you almost nothing. If you can't trace a coffee to a farm, you can't know how it was grown or whether the grower got a fair deal.

Roasted by the tonne

Industrial batches are built for uniformity at scale, not character. The subtle things that make a coffee remarkable are the first to get flattened.

One-harvest relationships

When buyers chase the cheapest lot each season, farmers can't plan ahead or invest in quality. Short-term buying makes exceptional coffee harder to grow.

How it works

Three steps from farm to cup. Nothing hidden in between.

We kept the chain short on purpose. Fewer hands between the grower and your grinder means better coffee and a clearer conscience.

STEP 01

We buy direct

We source from 12 smallholder farms through direct trade, and build long-term relationships with the people who grow our coffee.

STEP 02

We roast small

Every coffee is roasted in batches of 20kg or less in our Bristol railway arch, so each one gets our full attention.

STEP 03

You brew it your way

Subscribe and brew at home, find us in one of 40+ independent cafes, or pull up a stool at our brew bar.
