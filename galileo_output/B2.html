Why this exists

Most coffee can't tell you where it came from.

Too many bags say “premium” and nothing else — no farm, no grower, no story of how it was roasted. Somewhere between the hillside and the shelf, the things that make a coffee exceptional get blended out, scaled up and forgotten. Quarry Roast was built to keep them intact.

PN Priya NairFounder · Former UK Barista Champion

Before roasting a single batch, Priya spent three years visiting farms in Ethiopia, Colombia and Guatemala to understand what makes truly exceptional coffee. Quarry Roast opened in 2019.

Anonymous beans

Lots from countless farms get blended until nobody can say who grew what. When the origin disappears, so does the accountability.

Roasted by the tonne

Industrial roasting is built for uniformity, not character. The nuance a farmer spent a whole season growing is flattened in minutes.

One-season relationships

Buyers chase the cheapest lot each harvest, then move on. Farmers can't invest in quality when they don't know who's buying next year.

How it works

From farm to your cup in three deliberate steps.

No shortcuts, no middle layers we can't explain. Just a short, transparent line between the people who grow the coffee and the person who drinks it.

01

Sourced direct

We buy from 12 smallholder farms through direct trade, and we keep going back. Long-term relationships are how good coffee gets better.

02

Roasted small

Every coffee is roasted in batches of 20kg or less in our Bristol railway arch. Small enough that each one gets our full attention.

03

Brewed by you

Choose single origin, blends or espresso and have it delivered on subscription — or pull up a stool at our brew bar and taste it with us.

What we do

One roastery. Six ways to get closer to great coffee.

Whether you brew at home, run a cafe, or just want a properly good flat white on a Saturday morning, there's a way in.

Direct-trade sourcing

We source from 12 smallholder farms and build relationships that last beyond a single harvest. Transparent sourcing means we can tell you exactly where your coffee began.

Small-batch roasting

We never roast more than 20kg at a time. Small batches let us tune each roast to the coffee, not to a production schedule.

Coffee subscription

Single origin, blends or espresso, delivered from £15 a month. Pick the style that suits how you brew and we'll take care of the rest.

Wholesale supply

More than 40 independent cafes across the UK pour Quarry Roast. Every wholesale order gets the same sourcing and small-batch standards as our own bar.

Tasting room & brew bar

Open Wednesday to Sunday, 9am–5pm, in Bristol. Come in, taste what we're roasting and ask us anything.

Barista training

Courses run from complete beginner to professional level. Learn at a roastery founded by a former UK Barista Champion.
