Quarry Roast: Crafted with Deliberation

Our Story

Founded by Priya Nair, Quarry Roast is built on the principles of dedication to quality and transparency in sourcing. Our journey began with Priya's travels to Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala, immersing herself in the cultures and learning the secrets of exceptional coffee.

The Coffee

Our coffee sings with the voices of its origin. We roast in small batches to honor the unique flavors of each bean, a blend of warmth and richness that embodies the heart of the harvest.

For Café Owners
