Frequently Asked Questions

How fresh is your coffee when it arrives?

We roast to order and ship within 24 hours. Your coffee will typically arrive 2-5 days after its roast date, which is the perfect time to start brewing for optimal flavour.

Can I choose the coffee in my subscription?

Yes! For The Enthusiast plan, you can choose between our single origin, blend, or espresso profiles. The Explorer is a "Roaster's Choice" subscription, designed for discovering something new and exciting each month.

Do you offer different grind sizes?

Absolutely. You can choose whole bean or have your coffee ground for your specific brew method (e.g., French Press, V60, Espresso, AeroPress) at checkout.

What are your shipping policies?

We offer free standard UK shipping on all subscription orders and any orders over £25. For smaller orders, a small shipping fee applies. We ship via Royal Mail.

How does direct trade work?

It means we bypass traditional importers and exporters to build personal, long-term relationships directly with the farmers. We visit the farms, agree on a premium price far above market rates, and ensure the money goes straight to the people who deserve it.

Can I visit the roastery and tasting room?

We'd love to see you! Our Bristol railway arch tasting room and brew bar is open to the public from Wednesday to Sunday, 9am to 5pm. Come say hello!
