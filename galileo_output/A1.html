Frequently Asked Questions.

What makes Quarry Roast coffee special?

We focus on direct-trade relationships with smallholder farms, ensuring ethical sourcing and exceptional bean quality. Our small-batch roasting in Bristol then brings out the unique, nuanced flavours of each origin.

How do your subscriptions work?

Choose your preferred plan (Explorer, Connoisseur), grind type, and delivery frequency. We'll then send freshly roasted coffee directly to your door, ensuring you never run out of your favourite brew.

Can I visit your roastery?

Absolutely! Our tasting room and brew bar in our Bristol railway arch is open Wednesday to Sunday, 9am-5pm. Come say hello, enjoy a brew, and learn more about our process.

Do you offer barista training?

Yes, we offer a range of barista training courses, from beginner home brewing techniques to professional-level skills. Check our website for upcoming dates and availability.

What is direct-trade sourcing?

Direct-trade means we bypass intermediaries and buy directly from coffee farmers. This ensures farmers receive a fairer price, fosters long-term relationships, and gives us greater control over quality and transparency.
