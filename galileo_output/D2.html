The Problem

Why is finding great coffee so hard?

Most coffee is a commodity, passing through countless hands. The result is stale beans, murky ethics, and a total loss of what makes each origin special.

Opaque Supply Chains

It's nearly impossible to know where your coffee truly comes from, who grew it, and if they were paid fairly for their hard work.

Inconsistent Quality

Mass-produced coffee is roasted for shelf life, not flavour. This destroys delicate tasting notes and results in a bitter, one-dimensional brew.

Stale Supermarket Beans

Coffee is a fresh product. Beans sitting on a supermarket shelf for months have lost their vibrancy long before they reach your cup.

Our Process

Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the subscription work?

It's simple! Choose your plan, select your coffee preference (or let us surprise you), and tell us how you brew. We'll roast your coffee to order and ship it to you. You can easily manage your subscription, pause, or cancel from your account at any time.

When will I receive my coffee?

We roast every Monday and Tuesday. All orders, including subscriptions, are dispatched within 48 hours of roasting to ensure maximum freshness. You'll typically receive your coffee within 2-3 working days of dispatch.

Can I change the coffee in my subscription?

Absolutely. You can log into your account and swap your coffee selection for any other coffee we have in stock. You can also change your grind setting or the number of bags you receive.

Do you ship internationally?

Currently, we ship to the UK and most of Europe. Shipping costs for international orders are calculated at checkout. We are working on expanding our shipping options, so please check back!

What makes direct-trade different?

Direct-trade means we bypass traditional intermediaries and work directly with coffee producers. This allows us to pay them a significantly higher price, foster long-term sustainable relationships, and have complete transparency over the quality and ethics of our supply chain.
